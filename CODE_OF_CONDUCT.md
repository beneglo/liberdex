# Code of conduct

liberdex is a small project and this is a short document.

Be civil. Argue about the code, the numbers and the design, not about the
person. Assume the other side is acting in good faith until it is plain that
they are not. Review what was written, not who wrote it.

Not acceptable, anywhere the project is discussed: harassment, slurs,
threats, publishing someone's private details, sustained disruption of a
thread, or sexualised language and imagery.

Reports go to the maintainer through the contact on
[liberdex.net/imprint](https://liberdex.net/imprint) or by a private
vulnerability report on this repository if you prefer a channel that is not
email. Reports are read by one person and kept confidential. Anyone who
crosses the line may be warned, have comments removed, or be blocked from the
repository, at the maintainer's judgement.
