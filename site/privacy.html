Privacy

The site knows nothing about you, and the software runs on your machine.

This site

liberdex.net is static HTML served by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, loads nothing from third parties, runs no analytics, and the font is self-hosted. Your light or dark choice is kept in your browser's local storage and goes nowhere. GitHub, as the host, sees the requests it serves; its own privacy statement covers that. The site is run by the operator named in the imprint; for access, deletion or a complaint to a supervisory authority (Art. 15 to 21 and 77 GDPR), write to the address there.

The software

liberdex runs on the machine you install it on and sends no telemetry. A search goes to the model provider you configured for planning and to the sites the plan named, under your keys and your terms with them. Keys stay in ~/.config/liberdex/env.
